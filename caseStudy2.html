Overview

JessaKae is a fashion brand offering inclusive sizing and expressive designs. The website focuses on delivering a visually rich experience that connects emotionally with users while enabling smooth product discovery and browsing.

Challenges & Objectives

  • Maintaining performance with heavy visual content
  • Ensuring responsive consistency across devices
  • Balancing aesthetics with usability in product browsing

The objective was to deliver a smooth and responsive frontend experience while preserving the brand’s strong visual identity and storytelling approach.

Research & Strategy

The approach focused on analyzing modern e-commerce patterns and optimizing layout structures for better readability and navigation. Special attention was given to mobile usability and interaction flow.

Enhanced user flow for smoother browsing and interaction.
